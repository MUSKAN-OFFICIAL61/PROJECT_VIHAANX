## 01. Overview

Students build their professional identities across resumes, LinkedIn, GitHub, coding platforms, projects, and hackathons. However, these achievements remain scattered, making it difficult to understand how they translate into real career opportunities.

**SKILLORA** is an AI-powered career intelligence platform that consolidates professional information to evaluate skills, measure career readiness, identify skill gaps, and recommend actionable improvements.

It aims to answer three fundamental questions:

1. **Skill Authenticity:** Are your claimed skills supported by actual evidence?
2. **Career Readiness:** How prepared are you for a specific role or company?
3. **Growth Direction:** What should you improve to become a stronger candidate?

---

## 02. Problem Statement

Existing platforms typically address isolated aspects of career development, such as resume optimisation, coding performance, professional networking, or job discovery.

However, students still lack a unified system that connects their claimed skills, demonstrated abilities, and target career opportunities.

As a result, understanding actual skill proficiency, company-specific eligibility, and the improvements required to achieve career goals becomes challenging.

**SKILLORA bridges this gap through an integrated, evidence-based career intelligence ecosystem.**

---

## 03. Core Intelligence Engines

SKILLORA is built around three interconnected intelligence engines that form the foundation of its approach.

### 01 — Claim vs Proof Engine

**Transform self-declared skills into evidence-backed insights.**

Evaluates whether a candidate's declared skills are supported by available evidence from repositories, projects, coding platforms, and achievements.

**Key Capabilities**

- Claimed Skills vs. Demonstrated Skills Comparison
- GitHub Repository Analysis
- Coding Platform Performance Evaluation
- Project-Based Skill Validation
- Achievement Verification
- Evidence-Based Skill Assessment

**Verification Framework**

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Verified | Strong supporting evidence identified. |
| 🟡 Partially Supported | Relevant but limited evidence available. |
| ⚪ Unverified | Insufficient supporting evidence. |
| 🔵 Needs Review | Evidence is unclear or conflicting. |

### 02 — Career Readiness & Eligibility Engine

**Measure profile compatibility against real career requirements.**

Evaluates a candidate's professional profile against the requirements of a specific company, job role, or internship.

**Key Capabilities**

- Company-Specific Readiness Assessment
- Required vs. Available Skills Matching
- Academic Eligibility Verification (CGPA, Graduation Year)
- Project and Experience Evaluation
- Missing Qualification Identification
- Career Compatibility Score

**Compatibility Assessment**

The Career Compatibility Score is designed to consider:

- Technical Skills Match
- Verified Skill Evidence
- Academic Eligibility
- Relevant Project Experience
- Professional Experience

**Output:** An explainable readiness report highlighting candidate strengths, eligibility gaps, and areas requiring improvement.

### 03 — Opportunity-to-Readiness System

**Convert career opportunities into actionable improvement strategies.**

Connects a candidate's current capabilities with target opportunity requirements and generates a structured path towards becoming a stronger candidate.

**Key Capabilities**

- Target Opportunity-Based Gap Analysis
- Prioritised Skill Recommendations
- Relevant Project Suggestions
- Step-by-Step Improvement Roadmap
- Personalised Preparation Strategy
- Progress Tracking Through New Evidence
- Continuous Readiness Re-evaluation

**Output:** A personalised improvement pathway that helps candidates understand what to learn, what to build, and what to demonstrate to improve their compatibility with target opportunities.
